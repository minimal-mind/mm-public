# Venenum Tattoo Studio

## The Brief

Venenum is a tattoo studio with real presence — strong artists, a loyal following, and social media that genuinely reflected the quality of the work. The problem wasn't the business. It was everything digital that sat outside of Instagram.

The existing website was a WordPress build from a previous era. Nobody remembered who built it. Nobody knew how to maintain it. By early 2026 it had partially broken, and the owner — already busy running the studio — had neither the time nor the technical knowledge to fix it. She needed someone to take it off her hands entirely, not just patch it.

That's where we came in.

## The Problem

On the surface: a broken website that needed replacing.

Underneath: a brand that existed in fragments. The social media was doing its job — building trust, showing the work, drawing people in. But the moment a potential customer followed that trust to the website, it collapsed. Different fonts. Inconsistent colours. A layout that felt like it was built by someone who'd heard of branding but hadn't quite met it yet.

The branding wasn't wrong. It just hadn't been packaged. Nobody had sat down and said: here are the rules, here is the system, here is how it all holds together. The social content was gold. The website didn't know that.

The real problem was the trust gap. And a broken WordPress site was just the most visible symptom.

## The Approach

We weren't starting from scratch, and we didn't want to. The logo was professionally designed. The core colours were right. The social presence had already done the work of establishing a visual identity — we just needed to honour it.

The process was properly collaborative. We ran a design phase with the owner, working through her ideas and translating them into a coherent system. She had instincts about how the studio should feel online. Our job was to give those instincts structure — typography choices, spacing, colour application, hierarchy — and build something she could point people to with confidence.

We also made a deliberate infrastructure decision. WordPress, with its fragility and maintenance overhead, was gone. We brought Venenum into the MM stack: Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind, deployed on Vercel. Reliable. Fast. And no longer her problem to keep running.

## The Work

A full website rebuild and brand system, delivered as a single cohesive thing.

The site is a brochure-with-intent — every page designed to move visitors toward a contact form or a direct link to the studio. The artists each have their own page, with their portfolio work and Instagram links, giving clients the ability to choose who they want to work with before they even reach out.

A blog section rounds it out, giving the studio a space to communicate more broadly — beyond individual posts and stories.

On the brand side: we took the existing raw materials and codified them. Colour palette, typography, spacing rules — packaged into a system that could inform every touchpoint, not just the website. The social media already had its rhythm. The website finally matched it.

## The Outcome

The owner went from a broken site she couldn't touch to a clean, maintained digital presence she doesn't have to think about. That in itself was the win she needed most.

Beyond that: the studio now receives an average of 3–5 contact form enquiries per day through the site. The funnel works. People land, they browse the artists, they reach out.

The design process itself landed well. A few iterations, a couple of weeks of collaboration, and the owner ended up with something she was proud to show. More importantly — she now has confidence that the site reflects the quality of the work happening inside the studio. Which it does.

## Stack & Tools

- **Next.js** — framework
- **TypeScript** — type safety throughout
- **Tailwind CSS** — styling
- **Vercel** — deployment and hosting
- **Figma** — design and brand system
- **Resend** — contact form delivery
